# Finding and testing ideas

How successful Roblox developers find ideas and kill bad ones cheaply, plus a market snapshot. Use it when the
user has no idea yet, wants several to choose from, or asks what is trending; then score the candidates with the
SKILL.md table. Sources: developer videos 2026, Roblox's RDC 2026 discovery session, public chart/stat sites (list
at the end). **[docs]** = Roblox official. **[tut xN]** = N developers agree. **[snapshot]** = third-party data on
a date, re-check before relying on it.

## 1. Where winning ideas come from [tut x5]

- **Evolution beats revolution.** The biggest new games ride a trend wave (a meme, a hot genre, streamer chaos) and
  add a twist; brand-new mechanics need learning time players won't give. Start from something people already love
  and make it better or weirder. (A Dubit study is the commonly cited source.) [tut x3]
- **Roblox's own charts are free market research:** Top Trending (what's growing), Top Earning (what monetizes),
  Up-and-Coming, per-genre charts. Play the top 10 of a genre for a few minutes each and write down: how fast the fun
  starts, what players buy, why they'd come back tomorrow. [tut x3]
- **Mine the Home page:** play a few games of a niche and Home fills with similar ones, including 100-500 CCU games
  that never reach the charts; find what they lack (monetization, a better loop, updates, polish) and build the
  stronger rival. [tut x1]
- **Stat dashboards** (RoTrends, RoMonitor, rblxstat, bloxg, Rolimons) show genre up/down trends, CCU history and
  new releases; catch a rising sub-niche early (e.g. "tower" variants). [tut x1]
- **Outside Roblox:** most Roblox trends start on TikTok (memes, challenges such as viral food challenges turned into
  games); proven loops on Steam, CrazyGames/Poki browser games, Xbox Game Pass and the App Store with **demand
  signals**: millions of plays, hundreds of thousands of votes, creator videos about them, ideally no good Roblox
  version yet. [tut x2]
- **Core fantasy:** "what does a kid imagine before falling asleep?" (being a superhero, owning a zoo, stealing the
  rarest pet, escaping a monster). A clickable fantasy beats a clever system. [tut x1]
- **Clippable moments:** design for big wins, rare drops, chaos and fails that people film; creators market those
  for free. [tut x3]
- **One-sentence test:** explain the game to a stranger in one sentence; if you can't, it's too complex. Most Roblox
  games are too complex for the mass market: simple mechanics with depth win. [tut x2]
- **Names and SEO:** trend words in the title help search and click-through when players already know the meme
  (don't copy another game's name or images: non-unique metadata is demoted [docs]).

## 2. Testing an idea cheaply

1. **Package first** [docs + tut x3]: write the title, icon and thumbnail concept before building. Roblox: "the
   click gates every single other signal"; if you can't make a clickable thumbnail, the idea is weak. Optional: test
   several thumbnails on a minimal place with small ads; top studios even run ads to several copies of a game to
   test names. [docs: RDC 2026]
2. **MVP in ~2-4 weeks** [docs + tut x4]: one map, one core loop, the first reward fast, basic monetization and
   analytics funnels; no extras. Roblox's advice: launch MVPs and iterate instead of a year in the dark (but not a
   throwaway either). AI tools now help prototype the loop before a full team builds it. [tut x2]
3. **Private playtest:** watch friends or playtesters play without helping; every confusion is a fix. Roblox lets
   you invite a small group of age-checked testers before publishing (Audience > Limited/Playtesters). [docs + tut x1]
4. **Launch with small ads for about a week** and read the numbers (`roblox-launch-kit/references/growth.md`):
   developer rules of thumb for ad cohorts: average playtime 10+ minutes, D1 ~12-15%, D7 ~2-3%, and first Home
   Recommendation impressions within 1-2 weeks. [tut x3]
5. **Decide fast:** fix the single worst metric and re-test; if after ~2 weeks of fixes the numbers and Home
   impressions don't move, move on to the next idea and keep what worked (systems, art, audience). Studios treat
   ideas as a portfolio: many cheap tests, double down on the one that sticks. [tut x3]

## 3. Market snapshot, 2 November 2026 [snapshot]

Live counts from public stat sites (rblxstat.com, bloxg.com) on 2026-11-02; they move hourly.
- **Most played:** Steal An Egg (~850k-990k live, record 12.4M, created July 2026), Brookhaven RP (~370-530k),
  Murder Mystery 2 (~350-460k), Blox Fruits (~200-490k), Adopt Me! (~210k), 99 Nights in the Forest (~160-190k),
  RIVALS (~175k), Break and Steal an Egg (~170k, created Sept 2026), DONT SPLASH (obby, created 17 Oct 2026,
  ~75-110k), Jujutsu Shenanigans (~77-110k), Anime Adventures, Attack on Titan Revolution.
- **What it says:**
  - the "steal"/collect-and-defend formula is still the biggest wave (now with eggs/pets), and follow-ups with a twist
    reach six figures within weeks;
  - "**+1 X**" incremental simulators (+1 Spider Arm, +1 Ride Pets to Fight, +1 Anime Eyes) are a rising format: one
    stat that grows every second, a silly fantasy, a short title;
  - physics sandboxes (ragdoll playgrounds, Backseat Rally) and absurd premises (Teach Your Granny How To Drive)
    trend through clips;
  - cozy idle/farm games ("My Wood Farm", "Chicken Farm", "Pickaxe Tycoon") hold 99% like ratios at small CCU:
    loved, but small;
  - anime dice/gacha-style collection (Anime Dice ~50-70k) and anime RPGs stay strong;
  - classics (Brookhaven, MM2, Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits) keep the long tail; competing head-on is a trap;
  - survival-with-friends (99 Nights) and asymmetric horror (Violence District) remain big.
- Genre totals on that day (bloxg, live players across tracked games): simulators ~1.5-1.6M, tycoons ~1.9-2.0M,
  survival ~1.1-1.4M, roleplay ~0.8-1.2M, fighting ~0.6-0.9M, anime ~0.7-0.9M, RPG ~0.6-0.75M, horror ~0.5-0.64M;
  puzzle and story games are the smallest (~65-105k).

Always re-check the charts on the day of the evaluation; a trend can peak and flood with clones within weeks.
