Our Services

01

Design, Construction and Prototyping

We turn your ideas into reality. We offer the creation of clothing patterns based on sketches or photos, size grading, and sewing the first prototypes.

02

Technological Consulting

We provide professional technological support in the selection of appropriate yarns, materials, and accessories, ensuring the final product meets the highest standards.

03

Ready-to-use Photo Database

We provide professional photos that you can immediately use on your website.

04

Knitting Production

We specialize in machine knitting, creating high-quality weaves. We produce a full range of knitwear: sweaters, cardigans, turtlenecks, and dresses, as well as winter and year-round accessories such as hats, scarves, and snoods.

05

Finishing, Branding and Packaging

We deliver a product 100% ready for sale. We provide services for sewing in labels (jacquard, sizing, care instructions) and decorations (e.g., applying patterns). Each batch undergoes strict quality control, followed by ironing, careful folding, packaging, and attaching commercial tags.
